Keep watching. Malmark helps keep your MyAnimeList progress in place.

Watch anime on Crunchyroll, check the detected anime and episode, and confirm when you want your MAL list updated. Keep Malmark open in Chrome’s sidebar or use the popup.

• Detect anime and episodes on Crunchyroll, with manual matching and season offsets when needed.
• Track watched sections, including playback speed changes. Skipping ahead does not count as watching.
• Resume partial episode progress after closing the browser.
• Review queued episode reminders even after autoplay moves on.
• Adjust your watched episode count manually.
• Mark Finished Airing entries completed and optionally give them a score after the final episode.
• Add other HTTPS websites yourself by choosing their title and episode elements and granting access to that domain.

Updates ask for confirmation by default. Optionally enable automatic progress for exactly the next episode of an anime already in Currently Watching. It checks fresh MAL progress and shows a four-second success notice; other cases and failed updates keep the confirmation flow. Completion and scoring remain manual. Sign in through MyAnimeList; store installations use Malmark’s public app registration and do not require you to create an API app.

Privacy: account tokens, settings, saved matches and playback checkpoints stay in this browser. Anime titles and searches are sent to the official MAL API for matching; approved episode counts, completion status and scores go to MAL. Partial progress expires after 30 days, with at most 100 episodes retained. No Malmark backend, analytics or advertising.

Requires a MyAnimeList account and access to the anime you watch. Crunchyroll may require a subscription. Video playback remains controlled by your browser and streaming provider. Other websites need manual setup, and compatibility depends on their page structure and accessible video player. Netflix is not a built-in integration.

Desktop Chrome 121 or later. Independent project; not affiliated with MyAnimeList or Crunchyroll. No streaming content is supplied or downloaded by Malmark.
