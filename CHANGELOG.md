# Changelog

User-facing changes in Malmark. The installed app includes its current release and up to three earlier versions under Settings → What’s new.

## Unreleased

### Added

- Find the latest changes and previous releases in Settings → What’s new. Click the version in the footer to open them directly.
- After an update, the footer highlights the new version until you open its release notes.

### Improved

- The popup’s branding header scrolls away while the navigation tabs stay visible, leaving more room for content.

## 0.2.0 — Your watchlist, closer

### Added

- My list shows your Currently Watching titles, episode progress, and links to MyAnimeList. English titles appear first when available.
- Edit custom website selectors in the picker or under Sites, with previews and validation before saving.
- On-page prompts help you match an anime or grant access to an embedded player.

### Improved

- Opening Profile refreshes your MyAnimeList statistics and keeps saved statistics visible while loading.
- Settings lives in the header, and expanding the sidebar preserves your current view.
- Unmatched titles try shorter searches while retaining season and part information.
- Custom website setup continues after granting permission, and generated selectors use more stable surrounding elements.

### Compatibility

- Firefox requires desktop version 142 or later.

## 0.1.1 — More reliable anime matching

### Fixed

- Manual and automatic matching can find titles hidden by MyAnimeList’s default search filter, including Magical★Explorer.
